# PureScan voice and positioning

## Positioning (2026-09-26)

PureScan **informs; it never advises.** We tell people exactly what is in their
food and what the evidence says, and it is up to them what they do with it.
Transparency is the product: the same published method for every product, every
rating sourced, nothing hidden, nothing sponsored.

One-line: **"The honest truth about what's in your food. What you do with it is up to you."**

## Rules for every word we write (app, site, App Store, social, AI chat)

1. **Describe, don't prescribe.** State facts and evidence. Never tell people to
   eat, avoid, limit, choose, swap, cut down or "be careful".
   - No: "Best avoided", "Swap to…", "You should limit this", "a healthier choice".
   - Yes: "High in sugars", "IARC classes 4-MEI as possibly carcinogenic (Group 2B)",
     "Scores 68 points higher".
2. **Attribute judgements to evidence.** "EFSA set an acceptable daily intake of…",
   not "this is dangerous".
3. **No fear, no hype.** No "toxic", "poison", "chemicals", "clean", "shocking".
4. **Plain British English.** Short sentences. Sentence case.
5. **Personal settings are the user's choice.** The watch list records what *they*
   want to hear about. We never imply they should watch for anything.
6. **Always true about limits.** The data comes from Open Food Facts and can be
   wrong or out of date. Say so where it matters.

## Vocabulary

| Concept (code name) | Say | Don't say |
|---|---|---|
| Concern tier `high` | **High concern** (chip: "High") | Avoid |
| Concern tier `moderate` | **Moderate concern** (chip: "Moderate") | Limit |
| Concern tier `low` | **Low concern** (chip: "Low") | Minor |
| Concern tier `none` | **No known concern** | Safe, harmless |
| Verdict ≥75 / 50–74 / 25–49 / <25 | **Great / Okay / Poor / Bad** (a rating of the product, not the person) | Healthy / unhealthy |
| Verdict headline | Scores highly / A mixed picture / Scores low / Scores very low | A great choice / Best avoided / Best kept occasional |
| Swaps | **Higher-scoring alternatives** ("Alternatives") | Better swaps, healthier swaps |
| Avoid list | **Watch list** ("Tell me when a product contains…") | Avoid list |
| Findings section | **What's inside** | What to know, things to avoid |
| Score cap note | "Capped at 40: contains 3 ingredients of moderate concern" | "Held down: contains ingredients we suggest limiting" |
| AI chat | **Ask PureScan** | AI nutritionist, health coach |

## Standard disclaimer

"PureScan tells you what's in your food and what the evidence says. It isn't
medical or dietary advice, and what you do with it is up to you."
